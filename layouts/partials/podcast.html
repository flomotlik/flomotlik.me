The week in AWS, in the time it takes to make coffee

AWS ships hundreds of announcements a month and almost none of them come with a note explaining whether they matter to you. So I built a pipeline that reads every one of them each week, writes up what actually changed, and publishes it as a short episode — narrated by an AI model of my own voice.

  • What actually changed — new services, quota and pricing shifts, deprecations you need on your roadmap.
  • Short and weekly — one episode, every week, no backlog to catch up on.
  • Built on the stack it covers — Lambda collects the feeds, Bedrock writes the summary, and the pipeline ships an episode every week without anyone touching it.
